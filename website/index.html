Offline-First Accounting System for Indian Small Businesses

Works from USB • No Internet Required • Double-Entry Accounting

📊 Double-Entry Accounting

Strict debit=credit enforcement. Trial balance always resolves to zero. Professional-grade accuracy.

💾 USB Portable

Run directly from USB drive. Works on any Windows PC. No installation required on host machine.

🔒 Data Safe

SQLite WAL mode, atomic writes, crash recovery. Auto backup on start/exit. Your data is protected.

📈 Reports That Matter

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Debtor Aging, Creditor Schedule. Export to PDF instantly.

🏢 Multi-Financial Year

Switch between financial years seamlessly. Keep your historical data organized and accessible.

⚡ Fast & Offline

Local server (localhost), no cloud dependency. Lightning-fast performance even on slow USB drives.
